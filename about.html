About Glacier National Park

Glacier National Park is located in northwestern Montana and is part of the National Park Service. The park covers more than one million acres and contains mountains, forests, lakes, and diverse wildlife.

Glacier is a popular destination for people who enjoy hiking and exploring nature. Some of the animals found in the park include mountain goats, bighorn sheep, elk, and bears.
