About Utah

Utah is known for its incredible scenery and many opportunities for outdoor recreation. The state has five national parks, beautiful mountains, deserts, lakes, and many hiking trails.

The purpose of this website is to introduce visitors to some of Utah's interesting places and encourage people to explore the natural beauty of the state.
